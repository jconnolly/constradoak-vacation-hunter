## 🎯 The 2-Ticket Florida Trick

**Concept:** instead of NY→Caribbean direct, fly NY→FLL/MIA on Spirit/Frontier ultra-cheap, then connect to Caribbean as separate ticket on JetBlue/AA/Spirit.

**Math:**
- JFK→FLL Spirit: $50–120 RT
- FLL→LIR/SXM/RTB/AUA: $250–450 RT
- **Combined: $300–570 RT** vs single ticket $500–700 RT
- Save $100–200/pp × 10 = **$1,000–2,000 group total**

**Risks:**
- Missed connection = no airline rebooking
- Buy ~6-hour buffer + travel insurance
- Checked bags don't auto-transfer
- 10 pax + 4yo = harder logistics

**When it works:** if you'd be flying out Thu Feb 11 anyway (extra buffer day in FL). When it doesn't: Sat Feb 13 tight schedule.
