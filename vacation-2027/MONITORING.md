# Presidents Day 2027 Family Vacation — Monitoring Notes

**Travel window:** ideal Sat 2027-02-13 → Sat 2027-02-20. Flex: outbound as early as Thu 2027-02-11, return as late as Sun 2027-02-21 (hard stop ~Feb 22). Min 7 nights.

**Party:** 10 pax = 6 adults + 4 kids (ages 4, 10). Two families + grandparents staying together.

**Departure airports** (preference order): ISP > JFK > LGA > EWR (only if big savings).

**Hard constraints:**
- 4+ BR, private pool, sleeps 10
- Walkable to beach + bars/restaurants — **OR short drive (<15min) at the right price**. Hard NO on 40-min drives to beach.
- Warm enough to swim in Feb
- Latin America or island preferred
- Multi-leg OK (e.g. 2 nights FL + 5 nights island) if economics work
- OK to rent cars; just don't want to live in them

**Vibe:** NOT snobby. Likes "a little grimy" — Rincon, PR style. NOT polished resort enclaves.

**Ruled out (by user):**
- Puerto Rico west / Rincon (overdone)
- DR, Jamaica, Bahamas (soft no — "I don't think")
- Colombia (soft no)

**Flagged but NOT killed (user kept open):**
- USVI — short-drive rule resurrects this. Cruz Bay villas <10min drive to walkable village + beaches. Rent 2 SUVs/Jeeps.
- TCI — $27-37k total; world-class beach. Only if budget is no object.

**Unexplored (agents in flight 2026-05-19):**
- Belize (Caye Caulker, Ambergris, Placencia)
- Roatán (West End)
- Nicaragua (San Juan del Sur)
- St. Martin/SXM, Antigua, Anguilla, St. Kitts/Nevis
- Barbados, St. Lucia, Grenada, Tobago, Dominica

**Researched 2026-05-19, dropped:**
- Cayman — wrong vibe (polished/expensive), $24-35k total, not grimy
- Nicaragua (San Juan del Sur) — perfect vibe but US State Dept **Level 3 advisory** ("Reconsider Travel"), insurance complications, healthcare-thin. Wrong risk profile w/ 4yo + grandparents. Revisit if advisory de-escalates.
- Anguilla — luxury enclave, no walkable town, $15-40k+ villas
- Dominica — adventure island not beach-walkable, 9hr+ travel, no inventory
- Nevis — too quiet, "liming" vibe doesn't match grimy-walkable bar town
