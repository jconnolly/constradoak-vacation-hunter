### Direct comparisons you asked about (now anchored to Rincón)

- **Roatán vs Rincón:** **SAVE $2,800/family (~$1,400 each for mom + dad), −38%**. Better vibe (English Caribbean dive-town funky), 1-stop flight is the tradeoff.
- **Barbados vs Rincón:** **SAVE $2,832/family, −44%**. Direct JFK. Cheapest credible option.
- **St. Martin vs Rincón:** **SAVE $1,632/family, −25%**. Direct JFK, 5 daily nonstops. Best Caribbean foodie+walkable swap.
- **PR East (Palmas) vs Rincón:** **SAVE $1,432/family, −22%**. Same passport-free, same flights, different coast, gated resort with kids' beach club.
- **San Juan vs Rincón:** **+$568/family (+9%)**. Different vacation — urban beach + restaurants vs surf town. Villa stock is thin for 4BR+pool sleeps 10.
- **Tamarindo vs Rincón:** **+$968/family (+16%)**. Nonstop JFK→LIR, peak dry-season weather guarantee, premium villas. Worth it if a rainout would tank the trip.
- **Aruba vs Rincón:** **dead even on cost (+$0/family)**. More polished resort vibe vs surf town. Same passport hurdle.
- **TCI vs Rincón:** +$6,400/family (+100%). Hard skip.

### Key callouts

- **Cheapest direct-JFK + Rincón-vibe match: Barbados (St. Lawrence Gap)** or **Roatán** (1-stop).
- **Flight cost is NOT the big differentiator.** Most $4–6k group flights. Villa is what moves total.
- **Tight pricing (real quotes):** Roatán, Tamarindo, Aruba, Playa del Carmen, Rincón, San Juan.
- **Looser bands (estimates):** Curaçao, Bocas, Caye Caulker (thin inventory makes pricing volatile).

## What I'd Actually Do

Now that we have Rincón as our anchor (~$16k / $6,432 per family), the picture is sharper:

- **Roatán** = save $2,432/family (−38%) for a different funky walkable beach town. Tradeoff: 1-stop flight + Honduras less familiar.
- **St. Martin** = save $1,632/family (−25%) AND get nonstop + 5 daily JFK options + walkable French foodie strip. **Most attractive trade.**
- **Tamarindo** = pay $968/family more (+15%) for nonstop + dry-season weather guarantee + premium walkable surf town. The "if we want luxury upgrade" pick.
- **Aruba** = same money as Rincón but resort-strip vibe. Skip — pay same for less vibe match.
- **Barbados** = save $2,832/family (−44%), direct JFK, walkable strip. **Maximum savings, brand-new place.**
- **San Juan** = +$568/family for urban PR. Different vacation, not a Rincón replacement.

**Top 3 directions to put to the family:**

1. **"Cheaper than Rincón, new place" → Roatán or Barbados or St. Martin** (−25% to −44%)
2. **"Same money as Rincón, totally new" → St. Martin** (−25%, easiest logistics) or Aruba (±0%, less vibe match)
3. **"Spend a bit more, weather-bulletproof" → Tamarindo** (+15%)

For any of these we need to move in the next month. Peak-Feb 4-bedroom pool houses that sleep 10 are scarce and book out by October. Flights — late summer is the sweet spot.
